Privacy policy

What we collect when you use this website, why we collect it and how we look after it. Last updated 29 October 2026.

1. Who we are

The Breakwater Initiative ("Breakwater", "we", "us") is a fiscally sponsored project of RF Catalytic Capital, a subsidiary of The Rockefeller Foundation. RF Catalytic Capital is the legal entity responsible for the information described in this policy, and The Rockefeller Foundation's privacy policy applies alongside it. Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@breakwaterinitiative.org.

2. What we collect

Contact form. When you write to us through the contact page we collect your name, your email address and your message.

Newsletter. When you sign up for our briefing we collect your email address.

How the site is used. We use Google Analytics to understand which pages people visit, how they arrive at the site and what kind of device they use. Google Analytics records this with cookies and an anonymised identifier; it does not tell us who you are.

Server logs. Like every website, our hosting provider keeps short-lived logs of requests to the site, including the IP address they came from, for security and to keep the site running.

3. How we use it

We use your contact details to reply to you, and your newsletter address to send you the briefing you asked for. We use analytics to improve the site and to understand which of our work people find useful. We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who handles it for us

The site is built and hosted on Webflow, which stores form submissions and serves the site from servers in the United States. Google provides the analytics described above. The newsletter is sent through our email service provider, which holds subscriber addresses on our behalf. Each of these companies may only use your information to provide its service to us. As our fiscal sponsor, RF Catalytic Capital may also have access to information we hold.

5. Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies, named _ga and _ga_ followed by an identifier, that last up to two years and let it tell one visit from another. The site sets no advertising cookies and no other tracking cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings, or stop Google Analytics in every website by installing Google's opt-out add-on. The site works without cookies.

6. How long we keep it

We keep messages sent through the contact form for up to two years so that we can follow up on them. We keep your newsletter address until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is kept for fourteen months, the shortest period Google Analytics offers, and only in a form that does not identify you.

7. Your choices and rights

Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it by writing to hello@breakwaterinitiative.org. We will respond within a month. If you are in a place whose law gives you further rights over your personal information, those rights apply, and we will honour them.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update this page if what we collect or how we use it changes. The date at the top shows when it was last reviewed.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to hello@breakwaterinitiative.org or through the contact page.
