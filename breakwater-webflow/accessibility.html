Accessibility

The Breakwater Initiative wants everyone to be able to read and use this website, whatever their abilities or the technology they rely on. Last reviewed 29 October 2026.

What we are working towards

We are working towards the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.1, level AA. The site is built to be usable with a keyboard alone, to work with screen readers, to respect your reduced-motion setting, and to keep text readable at any size.

How we check

Every page is checked against the WCAG 2.1 AA criteria with automated tools before it is published, and we review the parts those tools cannot judge, such as the contrast of text over photographs, by hand.

Tell us what is not working

Accessibility is ongoing work rather than a finished job. If something on this site is hard to use, or you would like content in another format, please tell us through the contact page or by emailing hello@breakwaterinitiative.org. We aim to reply within five working days and to fix problems as quickly as we can.
